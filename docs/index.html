How a remittance reaches the school.

  1. The parent pays on Ethereum

    USDC goes to a gateway with a purpose attached: tuition, dormitory or books.

  2. Creditcoin reads the deposit

    Once Ethereum's block is attested, about seven minutes later, the Attestcoin Protocol proves the payment happened and succeeded.

  3. The school is paid locally

    A settlement pool on Creditcoin pays the registered school. A purpose the school does not accept is refused.

What the proof does not cover.

Attestcoin proves that a payment happened. Some of the story sits outside that.

  • Purpose is self-reportedThe gateway records what the sender says the money is for. The proof shows the transfer happened, not that the label is true.
  • Identity is off-chainNothing on-chain ties a beneficiary to a real person, so a sender and a recipient could be the same someone.
  • Schools are approved by an operatorThat is a trusted role. Limiting payouts to registered addresses limits transfers, not collusion.
  • Only Ethereum is readable todayAttestcoin attests Ethereum and Sepolia and nothing else yet. Another chain is a registry entry, not a rewrite.
